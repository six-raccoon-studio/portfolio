FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, the hosting account and your Google listing are all in your name. We get access to work on them, nothing more.

I already have an old website. Can you redo it?

Yes. We'll need the login for the account where your domain was bought. If nobody remembers it, we'll help you get it back.

Can customers order online?

Yes. We add an "Order online" button that opens your Uber Eats, DoorDash or Skip page, so orders come in the way you already handle them.

Do I need to know anything about tech?

No. You send us your menu, photos and hours. We do the rest.

How do I pay?

By e-transfer. Half when we start, half when your site goes live. Nothing until you've seen and liked your preview.

How long does it take?

Most one-page sites are ready to preview within a week of getting your menu and photos.

Call, text, or fill out the form. If you don't like the preview, you don't pay. Simple.
